# Business Case Study — B2B Sales Intelligence

## Business challenge

Incomplete briefings and inconsistent commercial rules can undermine proposal quality.

## Solution

Opportunity readiness assessment, gap analysis, commercial validation, human approval and DOCX proposal generation.

## Competencies demonstrated

Sales operations, business rules, AI-assisted structuring, process design.

## Documented evidence

The README documents a complete flow validated with a fictitious opportunity and a v1.0.0 technical core.

## Scope and limitations

No measured conversion improvement or production CRM integration.
